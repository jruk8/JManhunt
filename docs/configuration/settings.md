# Settings

> For: intermediate admins. Every page keeps its YAML blocks and per-feature headers.

Settings modify the match flow, tuned in-game through `/manhunt
config` or the [admin GUI](../play/gui.md). A fresh `config.yml`
with every default and comment generates on first run.

This section is split into focused pages:

- [Match Start & End](settings/match-start-end.md): autostart, the
  pre-start window, head starts, end delay, reminders, and disconnects.
- [Compass Tracking](settings/compass-tracking.md): targets, who gets
  one, refreshes, feedback, and distances.
- [Compass Advanced](settings/compass-advanced.md): analysis, signal
  interference, inaccuracy drift, and the navwand toggle.
- [Roles](settings/roles.md): resets, leave destination, spawn camping,
  friendly fire, lives and respawns, and invulnerability.
- [Win Conditions & End Screen](settings/win-conditions-end-screen.md):
  what wins a match and which statistics show afterwards.
- [Game Boosts](settings/game-boosts.md): world generation and loot odds
  that favor the speedrunners.
